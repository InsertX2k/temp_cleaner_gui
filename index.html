Free up the disk space you didn't know you could.

Temp_Cleaner GUI is a free, open-source cleaner for Windows. Tick the areas you want cleaned, then click "Start Cleaning".

Download for Windows View source on GitHub

Works on Windows 7 SP1[2] through Windows 11, and Windows Server 2012 onwards.

Why Temp_Cleaner GUI?

Free and open-source
Share it and use it for both personal and commercial purposes. No ads in the interface[1].
Easy to use
Check the areas you want to clean and click "Start Cleaning". That's all.
Supports most Windows versions
Compatibility is a top priority, so the vast majority of Windows desktop versions are supported.
Cleans safely
Built so it doesn't hurt the performance or stability of your PC or the programs on it.
Cleans what other cleaners can't
Many cleaning options that aren't available in most popular PC cleaners.
Cleans up your Android Smartphone too![0]
Thanks to the open nature of Android[0], and my expertise in Android[0] modding, this app is one of the few (if not the only one) that can also clean up your Android[0] smartphone alongside your PC!
Respects your privacy
No personal identification data is collected. See exactly what is.
Built-in updater
Introduced in v5.6-stable. Turn on Automatic Update Check in Settings and you'll be told when an update is out.
Active support community
Get help on the dedicated Discord server.
Trusted by reputable software sites
Listed on Softpedia, MajorGeeks and OlderGeeks.

[0] - This feature works by integrating a 3rd-party app, the Android Debug Bridge, that communicates with your Android Smart Phone via the proprietary ADB protocol.
